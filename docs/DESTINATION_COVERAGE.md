# Destination coverage and article rotation

**Editorial goal:** Give the magazine's eight weekly stories meaningful breadth across every Tranquilitas country and territory. Rotate locations over successive weeks; eight weekly articles does not mean eight thin country pages or a claim that every market offers immediate bookings.

**Publishing rule:** This is a research and coverage list, **not** an authoritative current list of locations where therapists are active. A public website, partner discussion, or quoted price is not proof of a bookable appointment. Before naming an active location, verify the current service page, partner status, appointment requirements, geographic coverage, and date. If service is not established, cover destination tourism or a prospective business opportunity without presenting Tranquilitas as operating there.

## Core coverage

- **The Bahamas:** Nassau, Paradise Island, New Providence and relevant tourism experiences; operational spa location #7 Fourth Terrace, Collins Avenue, Nassau.
- **Caribbean destination and territory rotation:** Turks and Caicos, Antigua and Barbuda, Grenada, St Kitts and Nevis, Barbados, St Lucia, Anguilla, British Virgin Islands (including Virgin Gorda), Belize, Cayman Islands, Curaçao, St Barthélemy, United States Virgin Islands (St Thomas/St Croix), Bonaire, Sint Maarten/St Martin, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (including Bequia), Dominica, Aruba. Add or remove destinations only on evidence; these currently have different partner/service-readiness states.
- **United States:** Las Vegas, Los Angeles and Orlando were confirmed as partner locations by Tranquilitas management. Other US markets such as Miami, Palm Beach and New York remain expansion research unless newer confirmation is obtained.
- **Spain:** Ibiza partner reported moving forward (MIMI Ibiza); research Mallorca, Marbella, Barcelona and Madrid distinctly and do not assume the same partner serves all.
- **Italy:** Milan partner reported moving forward (Lomi Lomi Milano). Rome and other locations require partner/fulfillment confirmation.
- **France:** Paris, Nice, Cannes, Provence and Bordeaux can support destination research or enquiry-first content; do not imply independently verified operating therapist networks.
- **United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom and Mexico:** planned or outreach markets only unless new evidence establishes confirmed service.

## Per-article gate

1. Choose a fresh destination/topic, check recently published slugs and this list, and prioritize an undercovered market.
2. Provide genuinely useful guest or industry information, not duplicate destination pages with swapped place names.
3. Distinguish company-reported partner arrangements, confirmed booking availability, enquiries, plans and independent evidence.
4. Verify current services/availability/prices and link to the **appropriate official destination website** when established; otherwise use a safe general enquiry page.
5. Give Tranquilitas a prominent and **clearly publisher-affiliated** role only where materially relevant. Never imply third-party editorial awards, exclusive resort relationships, guaranteed response times, unverified rankings, or service fulfillment.
6. Publish only after content, photo rights, metadata, sitemap, tests and live URL verification. Report blocked attempts as unpublished.

**Mix recommendation for eight pieces weekly:** 3 practical destination/guest guides, 2 wellness service/operator features, 2 evidence-backed tourism/business stories, 1 timely founder/innovation or market-expansion report. Adjust based on actual newsworthiness and sources. Track new coverage in the published story index rather than inventing activity data.
